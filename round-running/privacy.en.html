Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

This policy applies to the Round Running iPhone app. The operator is Youngjo Jo (independent developer), and the effective date is 2026-10-29. For privacy or support requests, contact messfilm@gmail.com. The policy and support pages are Privacy Policy and Support.

On-device information and its purpose

The app stores workout preferences and history within its storage on your iPhone to configure workouts and show previous settings and sessions.

  • Preferences and saved workouts: On-device preferences store your workout names, warm-up/work/rest/cool-down durations, round counts, target BPM, selected workout, language, installed voice selection, and sound and display preferences.
  • Workout history: On-device workout history stores start dates and times, the time zone at the start, the original workout name and plan, target BPM, elapsed workout time, completed rounds, the last checkpoint, and statuses such as completed, stopped, or interrupted. Internal identifiers are used to manage records.
  • Session state: Records are updated during a workout. On relaunch, an unfinished previous record is marked interrupted. This does not mean the previous workout automatically resumes or that speech continues after the app has stopped running.

The current app has no app account, developer-server upload of settings or workout history, account-based synchronization, advertising, tracking, or developer analytics SDKs. It does not measure GPS location, heart rate, or calories, and does not record through the microphone. Target BPM is a user-selected rhythm, not sensor-measured health data. Voluntary support messages and Apple services are described separately below.

Speech and Apple device features

Voice guidance uses iOS speech synthesis and voices installed on the device. Additional voice downloads, storage, and management are handled by Apple/iOS settings and may require a network connection. Workout phrases are not sent to a developer-operated speech server. Synthesized guidance audio is reused in memory; the app does not record your voice through the microphone.

A workout Live Activity can display the workout name and progress on the lock screen and other system surfaces. Consider whether others can see personal workout names, and review your iOS Live Activity settings.

Retention and deletion

Preferences and workout records remain on your device until you change or delete them. The current app does not automatically delete them after a fixed retention period.

  • Delete a saved workout from My workouts in the workout list or from the workout editor, then confirm. Deleting the selected workout can end its current session. Deleting a saved plan does not delete historical records created from that plan.
  • Delete an individual historical record using its trash control in Settings → Workout history, then confirm. Expand grouped sessions to select an individual record. Finish an active workout before deleting its in-progress record.
  • Change preferences in Settings. The current app has no single control that resets all preferences, saved workouts, and history.
  • Choosing Delete App in iOS removes the app and its related local data. Offload App retains documents and data and is different from deleting them. We do not promise immediate permanent erasure from storage media or backup copies.

Device backups and restoration

Separate from any app-level account synchronization, app data may be included in iCloud or computer device backups. Inclusion, retention, and restoration depend on your backup settings and Apple features. Deleting information in the app or deleting the app does not automatically erase existing iCloud or computer backup copies. Manage those backups separately through the relevant Apple settings or computer.
