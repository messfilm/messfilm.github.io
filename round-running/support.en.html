Start a workout

In the Plans tab, tap + to create a plan, or add a preset to My workouts, then set and save the warm-up, work, rest, cool-down, and round settings. Tapping a plan selects it on the ready screen of the Run tab without starting it. On the Run tab's ready screen, swipe sideways to choose a workout and tap Start workout to begin. During a workout you can use Pause / Resume, the previous and next interval controls, and End workout. The Pacer tab calculates the speed and pace of each part from a target distance and time. Choose durations and effort that suit your experience and how you feel.

Quiet or missing audio

Stop somewhere safe. Check device media volume, connected headphones, cadence enabled/volume, and voice/cue enabled/volume. In-app levels do not directly change the device’s master volume or another music app’s volume. Cadence may pause during guidance and return afterward. Guidance uses installed iOS voices; manage additional voices in iOS settings.

Screen lock, calls, or headphone changes

While safely stopped, check playback/pause state, audio notices, and the output device. Live Activity numbers and audible playback are separate. Guidance does not continue after you force quit the app. On reopening, unfinished workout history is marked interrupted. If an old lock-screen activity remains, reopen the app to check its current state.

Is BPM my heart rate?

Target BPM is a chosen rhythm counting both feet, not a sensor measurement of cadence or heart rate. The app does not measure GPS distance, speed, or calories.

Delete workouts or history

Delete saved workouts by swiping a row left, or long-pressing it, in Plans → My workouts. Delete a history record by tapping it in the History tab to open Edit record and choosing Delete record, or delete records in bulk by period under Settings → History storage, where you can also turn on automatic deletion of records older than 6 months or 1 year (off by default). End an active workout before deleting its history. Workouts with less than 5 minutes of actual workout time (paused time not counted) aren't saved to History. Saved workouts and past history are deleted separately. There is no single button to erase all data. Deleting an app, offloading it, and deleting backups are different actions.
